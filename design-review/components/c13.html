Key takeaways

  • 01

    Inbound calls out-convert web leads roughly 8× because intent is already established — the prospect dialled you, so the qualification job is confirmation rather than persuasion.

  • 02

    A warm bridge protects the buyer's minutes. Screening for state, age band and coverage before the transfer keeps agent talk-time on billable conversations instead of on triage.

  • 03

    TCPA-compliant consent is the whole asset. One-to-one written consent with trusted-form capture is what makes a Medicare or Final Expense transfer sellable at all.

Why this matters Media cost is not the lever — accepted-transfer rate is. Two points of qualification lift moves more margin for an Auto Insurance or HVAC buyer than a ten-percent cut in CPC ever will.
