PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
PPC, Performance Marketing, Lead Generation, Inbound Calls &
Live Transfers Website
1. PROJECT SUMMARY
Build a modern, premium, professional website for a company operating in the PPC, Performance
Marketing, Lead Generation, Pay Per Call, Inbound Calls, and Live Transfer industry.
The website's primary purpose is to:
Attract potential business clients.
Showcase the company's customer acquisition capabilities.
Demonstrate expertise across multiple industry verticals.
Explain available acquisition products.
Generate qualified business inquiries.
Build topical authority through industry-specific product knowledge and SEO content.
The website must be structured as a scalable PPC and Customer Acquisition platform, not simply a
generic corporate website.
2. PRIMARY BUSINESS POSITIONING
The company provides customer acquisition solutions through:
PPC / Performance Marketing
Web Lead Generation
Inbound Calls
Qualified Calls
Live Transfers
Customer Acquisition Campaigns
The primary business message throughout the website should communicate:
We help businesses acquire high-intent customers through performance-driven marketing,
web leads, inbound calls, and live transfers.

7. VERTICAL ARCHITECTURE
IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE
The website must not create one generic "Inbound Calls" page and one generic "Live Transfers" page for all
industries.
Instead, every supported sub-vertical must have its own dedicated acquisition product pages.
Example:
Insurance
   ↓
Auto Insurance
   ├── Auto Insurance Inbound Calls
   └── Auto Insurance Live Transfers
Another example:
Home Services
   ↓
Pest Control
   ├── Pest Control Inbound Calls
   └── Pest Control Live Transfers
This structure allows:
Better SEO targeting.
Clearer client messaging.
More specific product information.
Scalable content.
